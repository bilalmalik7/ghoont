How it works

Three steps. Zero apps.

Most people don't skip water on purpose. They just get busy and forget. GHOONT prints the plan on the bottle, so you see it every time you look at your desk.

01

Fill it in the morning

Fill it to the top when you start your day, at home, at the office or before your class.

02

Drink to the line

Each line shows a time. At 12 PM, the water should be down to the 12 PM line. If you're behind, you'll see it straight away.

03

Done by evening

Empty by 8 PM means you've had your litres for the day. Refill and keep going if it's a hot day.

Questions

Good to know

Is the plastic safe for hot weather?
The bottles are BPA-free. Like any plastic bottle, don't leave it in a parked car in direct sun for hours, and don't fill it with boiling water.
Do the time markers wash off?
No. The markers are printed into the surface and survive normal hand washing. Use a bottle brush and avoid scouring pads on the printed side.
My day starts at 11, not 8. Will it work?
Yes. Treat the top line as "first sip" and keep the same 2-hour rhythm. The lines are a pace, not a strict clock.
How does delivery work?
Order on WhatsApp and we'll confirm your address and send it by courier. You pay cash when it arrives. Delivery times depend on your city, and we'll tell you when we confirm the order.
Can I order in bulk for my office, gym or school?
Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with the quantity. We can also print your logo on bulk orders.
